Road Player: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Road Player is a single-player game that works entirely on your device. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the game collects

Nothing. Road Player does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What is saved on your device

Your careers (progress, in-game bankroll, settings) are saved in the app's own storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game. It may be included in your own device backups, which are controlled by you and your device's settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The game is bought once through the App Store. There are no in-app purchases. The purchase itself is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the game never sees your payment details.

The money in the game

All betting in Road Player uses pretend in-game money that has no real-world value. Nothing can be bought, won or cashed out.

Children

Road Player is not directed at children. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact and support

Questions about this policy, or help with the game: adammiller@hey.com. The support page is at https://teamloop.co/roadplayer/support.html.
