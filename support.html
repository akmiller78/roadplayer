Road Player: Support

A pool hall hustling game for iPhone.

Get in touch

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea? Email adammiller@hey.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have, which iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I play?

The game has a full Player's Guide: in any room, tap More, then Guide. The Old Timer at Duffy's walks you through your first night, and you can hear his tips again from More, Settings.

Nobody will play me.

That usually means you've been winning too obviously in that room. Offer weight, lower the stakes, try a different room, or come back another night. People who never play for much won't play for much, whoever you are.

I'm broke.

You can sweep floors at Duffy's for a little money, find a backer, or start a new career. Going broke is part of the game.

The cards that pop up to offer bets are too frequent.

Turn off "Offer bets, raises and rounds as they come up" in Settings. Offers from your opponent and the rail will still stop the game, since they're waiting on your answer.

Is my progress backed up?

Careers are saved on your device and included in your normal device backups. The game itself has no accounts and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your careers.

Is any of the money real?

No. All betting uses pretend in-game money with no real-world value. There is nothing to buy inside the game.
